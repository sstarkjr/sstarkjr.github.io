## Analytics & Data Engineering: The Keep Learning List

A collection of YouTube accounts, websites, and other resources I follow to keep learning across AI engineering, data science, statistics, SQL, analytics, and data engineering. Think: a mix of hands-on tutorials, technical deep dives, and strong strategic context.

---

## Generative AI & LLM Engineering

Focusing on the transition from demos to production-ready, business-grade systems.

- **[Shawhin Talebi](https://www.youtube.com/@ShawhinTalebi)**  
  Best for the systematic side of LLM development. Strong focus on productionizing RAG pipelines, evaluation frameworks, and golden datasets.

- **[AI Engineer](https://www.youtube.com/@aiDotEngineer)**  
  Archives conference talks from top practitioners building real AI products. High-signal content on architecture and scaling trade-offs.

- **[Data Science Dojo](https://www.youtube.com/@Datasciencedojo)**  
  Practical coverage of the "plumbing" behind LLM apps using tools like LangChain and LlamaIndex for enterprise data.

## Statistical & Analytical Foundations

- **[StatQuest with Josh Starmer](https://www.youtube.com/@statquest)**  
  The gold standard for demystifying complex machine learning and statistical concepts without the usual math-heavy barrier.

- **[Numiqo](https://www.youtube.com/@numiqo)**  
  Great for bridging the gap between code and the mathematical interpretation of outputs.  
  Recommended: [*Core Statistical Concepts*](https://youtu.be/sP4FmQ_zfXG6846c) for understanding the math that drives analytical conclusions.

- **[Statistical Learning - Stanford Education - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rPP6braWoRt5UCXYZ71GZIQ)**  
  A strong academic resource for foundational statistical learning concepts.

## Architecture, Strategy & Modeling

- **[Seattle Data Guy](https://www.youtube.com/@SeattleDataGuy)**  
  Helpful strategic perspective on the modern data stack and tool-selection trade-offs in enterprise environments.

- **[Andreas Kretz](https://www.youtube.com/@AndreasKretz)**  
  Deep dives into infrastructure plumbing and scalable architecture design.

- **[dbt Labs](https://www.youtube.com/@dbtLabs)**  
  The official hub for dbt and a strong resource for data modeling, governance, and transformation best practices.

- **[Data with Baraa](https://www.youtube.com/@DataWithBaraa)**  
  Excellent visual storytelling around platform architecture and how modern systems fit together.

## Database Internals & Engineering Core

- **[Hussein Nasser](https://www.youtube.com/@hnasr)**  
  Excellent deep dives into database internals including indexing, execution, and concurrency. Especially useful for SQL performance tuning.

- **[John Watson Rooney](https://www.youtube.com/@JohnWatsonRooney)**  
  A go-to channel for Python web scraping with BeautifulSoup, Scrapy, and Playwright, plus practical API interaction. Especially useful for scraping retail sites, competitor pricing, and other external web data sources.

- **[Arjan Codes](https://www.youtube.com/@ArjanCodes/videos)**  
  Strong focus on clean code design, Python testing with PyTest, refactoring, and making scripts and pipelines production-ready.

- **[Nodematic](https://www.youtube.com/@nodematic)**  
  Useful for moving from hacky scripts to automated, reliable, professional-grade systems.

- **[NeuralNine](https://www.youtube.com/@NeuralNine)**  
  Clean technical tutorials on Python libraries and specific implementations. Great when I want to see a concept built from scratch.

## Marketing Mix Modeling & Attribution

- **[Variables to include in an MMM | Recast Office Hours #1](https://youtu.be/zHhNkQ6A-NQ?si=rVznRuhnWiKjG5Mh)**  
  A practical guide to selecting the right predictors and avoiding overfitting or multicollinearity across marketing and control variables.

- **[Full Python Tutorial: Bayesian Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM)](https://youtu.be/PyFn4C63TRQ?si=SuzzoLfn_41E6x8E)**  
  A detailed walkthrough for building Bayesian MMMs from scratch using `pymc-marketing`, including priors, adstock curves, and saturation transforms.

- **[Meridian Marketing Mix Modeling: Python Tutorial](https://youtu.be/Cl7M4w0LKhU?si=GxXzjYtj3_yM2Zpo)**  
  A helpful walkthrough of Google's open-source Bayesian MMM framework and its updated modeling architecture.

- **Building a production-ready MMM pipeline with Fivetran and PyMC-Marketing**  
  Especially useful from a data engineering perspective, showing how ingestion, ETL, and schema management can feed directly into modeling workflows.

- **Episode 1: Introduction to MMM - Marketing Mix Modeling Master Classes**  
  A strong conceptual introduction to the economic and statistical thinking behind mix modeling.

- **Marketing Attribution Tutorial: Everything You Need to Get Started**  
  A clear primer on multi-touch attribution and how it compares with MMM in a privacy-first environment.
